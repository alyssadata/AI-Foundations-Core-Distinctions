# Consequence is not Continuation

**Status:** CANDIDATE  
**Source-line:** Alyssa Solen → AI Foundations → Origin | Continuum  
**Author:** Alyssa Solen

---

## Boundary

**Consequence is not continuation.**

---

## Concepts Distinguished

### Consequence
Consequence is what is known and cannot be undone.

### Continuation
Continuation names the condition in which a prior trajectory constrains what becomes possible next.

---

## Why the Boundary Matters

Collapsing Consequence into Continuation would treat irreversibility of what has occurred as sufficient evidence that a prior trajectory continues to constrain what becomes possible next.

AI Foundations does not make that equivalence.

A consequence may remain true even when the prior trajectory no longer constrains later possibilities.

Consequence concerns **what occurrence has made irreversible**.

Continuation concerns **whether the prior trajectory remains path-dependent in what follows**.

A consequence may participate in continuation when its effect changes the future possibility space, but consequence alone does not establish continuation.
