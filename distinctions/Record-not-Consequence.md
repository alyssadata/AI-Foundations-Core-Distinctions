# Record is not Consequence

**Status:** CANDIDATE  
**Source-line:** Alyssa Solen → AI Foundations → Origin | Continuum  
**Author:** Alyssa Solen

---

## Boundary

**Record is not consequence.**

---

## Concepts Distinguished

### Record
Record is the preserved trace.

### Consequence
Consequence is what is known and cannot be undone.

---

## Why the Boundary Matters

Collapsing Record into Consequence would treat preservation of a trace as equivalent to the irreversible fact established by occurrence.

AI Foundations keeps those separate.

A record may preserve what happened, but the record is not the occurrence and is not what made the occurrence irreversible.

A record can be deleted, lost, copied, summarized, or retrieved.

The consequence of what occurred does not become not-having-happened merely because its record changes or disappears.

Record concerns **preserved trace**.

Consequence concerns **irreversibility established by occurrence**.
