## Promotion Rule

A distinction may be considered for Locked Canon only after:

1. it preserves a necessary boundary between canonical concepts;
2. collapsing the concepts would materially distort the framework;
3. it is not better classified as a definition, axiom, or structural relationship;
4. Alyssa Solen explicitly approves the distinction for lock; and
5. it is listed as **LOCKED** in the Locked Canon `CANONICAL_INDEX.md`.

Candidate status is a working status only. It creates no presumption of future lock.
